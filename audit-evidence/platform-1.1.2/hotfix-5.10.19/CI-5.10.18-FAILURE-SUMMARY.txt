Root cause 5.10.18:
5.10.18 pokryvala BFCache resume pres pagehide/pageshow persisted=true, ale ne druhy zpusob historie,
kdy Chromium vytvori novy dokument typu performance navigation 'back_forward'. V teto vetvi mohl Platform
1.1.2 replay probehnout behem inicializace nove stranky jeste pred tim, nez prohlizec obnovil hodnoty
formularovych poli z historie. Child tedy spravne vycistil storage a zapsal ACK, ale prohlizec pote obnovil
stary synteticky canary do DOM textarea/input. Proto byl jediny FAIL back-dom-not-restored.

Hotfix 5.10.19:
- detekuje fresh history traversal uz pri bootu pres PerformanceNavigationTiming.type=back_forward
  s legacy fallback performance.navigation.type=2;
- pred registraci replay handleru zapne lifecycle stav restoring;
- suite replay, storage guard a startup/focus/visibility guardy se behem history restore odlozi;
- po pageshow se cleanup naplanuje az za requestAnimationFrame + task boundary;
- cleanup je v history vetvi vynucen znovu nad skutecne obnovenym DOM;
- ACK zustava posledni krok az po cleanupu, storage verification a completion markerech;
- pridan deterministicky regresni scenar browser-history-fresh-navigation-replay.
